FJSS bundled typography

Bodoni Moda (variable TTF):
- BodoniModa.ttf — regular variable family, used for FJSS wordmark, movie titles,
  platform headings and clock.
- BodoniModa-Italic.ttf — italic variable family, bundled for future optional use.

Montserrat:
- Montserrat-Regular.otf — dates, descriptions and navigation labels.
- Montserrat-SemiBold.otf — navigation and emphasized UI labels.

All font binaries are bundled locally. The launcher loads them from Android assets
and does not depend on fonts installed by the television. Font families are licensed
under the SIL Open Font License; retain the accompanying OFL license files.
